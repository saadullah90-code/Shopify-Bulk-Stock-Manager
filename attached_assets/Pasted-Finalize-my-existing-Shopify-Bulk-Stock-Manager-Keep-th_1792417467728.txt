Finalize my existing Shopify Bulk Stock Manager. Keep the app simple, reliable, and connected directly to the working Shopify Admin API. Do not use the Replit Shopify Connector or OAuth.

### Required workflow

**1. Sold Out**
- I enter one or multiple Shopify order numbers.
- Find the exact product variants in those orders.
- Automatically enable inventory tracking if it is off.
- Set inventory to exactly 0 at all relevant Shopify locations, including locations with existing positive quantities.
- Verify that no positive sellable inventory remains at other locations that could make the variant appear in stock.
- Do not change unrelated variants.

**2. In Stock**
- I enter one or multiple order numbers and select In Stock.
- Find the exact variants associated with those orders.
- Disable inventory tracking for those variants, so Shopify can treat them as available without tracked stock, subject to product status, sales-channel availability, and Shopify settings.
- Do not invent or restore previous quantities.
- Do not change unrelated variants.

### Keep the UI simple
- One input box for multiple order numbers.
- Two actions: Sold Out and In Stock.
- No manual inventory-location dropdown.
- Show a read-only preview of the matched orders and variants.
- Require confirmation before applying changes.
- Show clear success, skipped, and failure results.

### Technical requirements
- Reuse the existing working `SHOPIFY_STORE_DOMAIN` and `SHOPIFY_ADMIN_ACCESS_TOKEN` secrets.
- Use the supported Shopify Admin GraphQL API version already configured in the project.
- Discover and handle inventory locations automatically on the backend.
- Use the correct Shopify mutations for tracking and inventory quantities.
- Handle pagination, duplicate variants, missing orders, inactive locations, API errors, and partial failures.
- Never expose credentials or show fake success messages.
- Do not claim an update succeeded unless Shopify confirms it.
